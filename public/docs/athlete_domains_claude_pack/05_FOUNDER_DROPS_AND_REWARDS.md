# Founder Drops and Community Rewards

## Goal
Reward the original Athlete Domains / Discord community in a way that:
- feels valuable
- drives attention to the app
- stays App Store safe
- reinforces long-term loyalty

## Founder Program Name Ideas
- Athlete Domains Founders
- Athlete Domains OG Collection
- Athlete Domains Legacy Holders
- Athlete Domains First Team

## Safe Founder Benefits
### Community Status
- OG Discord role
- founder badge
- special founder announcement channel

### App-Adjacent Benefits
- beta access to new features
- early access announcements
- founder badge in profile or locker room
- founder-themed cosmetic frame or label if implemented broadly and safely

### Broadly Available In-App Benefits
If you choose to give packs, coins, or cosmetics as part of marketing campaigns, make sure those same reward types are also available through:
- gameplay
- ads
- standard promotions
- standard iOS purchases

NFT ownership should not be the exclusive entitlement path.

## Founder Drop Plan
### Stage 1
Reactivate Discord and announce the comeback story.

### Stage 2
Announce the Founder Program and future collection direction.

### Stage 3
Launch a founder badge / founder recognition system.

### Stage 4
Offer early windows for future announcements, testing, or cosmetic previews.

## Messaging Theme
Use this theme:
- you were early once
- the ecosystem is now becoming real products
- founder identity carries forward
- this time the products are playable now and expandable later

## What To Avoid In Messaging
Avoid saying:
- buy NFT to unlock app items
- NFT holders get exclusive app currency
- only NFT holders can use certain skins in iOS

## Recommended Sequence
1. relaunch Discord messaging
2. tease founder recognition
3. launch app update with locker room and starter avatars
4. follow with safe founder identity layer
5. introduce NFT collection as future-facing collectible identity
