# NFT Bridge Strategy (App Store Safe)

## Goal
Allow the 3,000 Athlete Domains NFT collection to launch for future Hytopia / ecosystem utility while providing present-day value around the iOS app without making the app dependent on NFT ownership.

## Core Rule
The app must work completely without NFT ownership.

## NFT Positioning
The NFT represents:
- digital collectible athlete identity
- future Hytopia identity / utility
- long-term ecosystem collectible
- founder / community status

## iOS App Positioning
The app represents:
- playable avatar usage
- cosmetic customization
- pack opening economy
- monetization via iOS-safe channels

## Safe Relationship Between NFT and App
Use a parallel model, not a gating model.

### Safe Statements
- this NFT represents an Athlete Domains identity with future ecosystem utility
- NFT holders may receive community recognition, early communication, and early access opportunities
- the mobile game has its own unlock paths through gameplay, rewards, and in-app purchases

### Unsafe Statements
- NFT ownership unlocks this iOS skin
- NFT rarity grants iOS coins
- NFT holders are the only users who can use this avatar in the app
- buy the NFT to get value inside the app

## Safe Benefit Types For NFT Holders
### Community Benefits
- Discord OG role
- founder recognition
- access to community polls
- early news on future drops

### Timing Benefits
- early access to announcements
- early access window for future cosmetic drops that later open to all users
- beta testing priority

### Identity Benefits
- founder badge in community spaces
- optional founder badge in-app if not tied to gameplay power or exclusive app entitlements

## Risky / Avoided Benefit Types
- direct coin grants based solely on NFT ownership
- direct avatar unlocks based solely on NFT ownership
- premium gameplay power
- bypassing App Store payment rails

## Recommended Public Framing
The NFT collection should be sold for:
- future Hytopia utility
- collectible ownership
- founder identity in the Athlete Domains ecosystem

The iOS app should be marketed independently as:
- a game with cosmetic collection systems
- a pack and customization economy
- an app that any user can enjoy without crypto or NFT ownership

## Long-Term Path
### Phase 1
Launch iOS app economy and founder badge systems.

### Phase 2
Launch NFT collection and community founder positioning.

### Phase 3
Use off-app systems to map NFT to identity and future Hytopia utility.

### Phase 4
If external platforms allow, expand cross-platform identity features outside the iOS unlock flow.
